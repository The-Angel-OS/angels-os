## I. Civil RICO — 18 U.S.C. § 1964(c)

### Elements

To prevail on a civil RICO claim, plaintiff must establish:

1. **Conduct** of an **enterprise** (2+ persons)
2. Through a **pattern** of **racketeering activity** (≥2 predicate acts within 10 years)
3. **Causation** — plaintiff's injury was caused by the racketeering
4. **Damages** — treble damages available, plus attorney's fees

### B. Predicate Acts — Pattern of Racketeering Activity

**Wire Fraud — 18 U.S.C. § 1343**  
Each utility bill or service charge transmitted electronically that misrepresented the cost basis constitutes a separate wire fraud predicate act.

Specific instances:
- Master-meter electricity resold at retail rates while Doug purchased at bulk commercial rates — each monthly billing cycle = a wire fraud predicate act
- CSC ServiceWorks app-based laundry billing — conversion from coin-op to app without notice or credit for existing balances; electronic charges for services affirmatively blocked
- HVAC maintenance charges invoiced for capital improvements that are landlord's statutory obligation

**Mail Fraud — 18 U.S.C. § 1341**  
Any written lease terms, renewal notices, or billing statements sent by mail or email that misrepresented the utility arrangement constitute mail fraud predicates.

**Extortion — 18 U.S.C. § 1951 (Hobbs Act)**  
The retaliatory eviction notice issued within 6 months of written habitability complaints, by a licensed real-estate attorney who knows the eviction is legally defective, constitutes attempted extortion under color of right — using fear of eviction to suppress legitimate tenant complaints and obtain continued rental payments under false pretenses.

**Count of Predicate Acts:**
- 9 years × 12 monthly utility billing cycles = **108 potential wire fraud predicates**
- Each written notice (eviction, lease renewal, billing statements) = additional mail fraud predicates
- Minimum 2 predicates within 10 years: **easily satisfied**

**Relatedness and Continuity:**  
All predicate acts relate to the same property, the same enterprise, the same victims, and the same scheme. The 9-year duration and ongoing nature of the billing conduct satisfies both the "closed-ended" and "open-ended" continuity tests per *H.J. Inc. v. Northwestern Bell*, 492 U.S. 229 (1989).

## II. Florida Civil Conspiracy

**Elements (Florida law):**
1. Agreement between two or more parties
2. To do an unlawful act or a lawful act by unlawful means
3. Performance of some overt act in furtherance of the conspiracy
4. Damage to plaintiff as a result

**Theory:**  
Douglas Hilkert (individually) and Hilkert Realty Holdings LLC (and any additional entities) agreed to:
- Operate a master-meter utility resale scheme without FPSC registration
- Maintain substandard habitability conditions while collecting full rent
- Use the eviction process as a threat mechanism to suppress complaints

Each LLC filing, each utility bill, each eviction notice is an overt act in furtherance of this agreement.

**Why this matters beyond the individual counts:** Civil conspiracy allows plaintiff to hold ALL members of the enterprise jointly and severally liable for the entire damages, including acts of co-conspirators. If Doug transferred assets to additional LLCs before or during litigation, conspiracy liability pierces those entities.

## III. Lis Pendens — Critical Timing Note

The Colliers/Crexi listing at $2.7M (documented in `OWNERSHIP_PROVEN.md`) creates an urgent asset dissipation risk. Under Florida law:

- **Lis pendens (F.S. 48.23):** Must be filed simultaneously with the complaint. It clouds the title and prevents a good-faith purchaser defense.
- **Fraudulent Transfer (F.S. 726):** If assets are conveyed to a related entity or family member after demand is made and before suit, the transfer may be voided.
- **RICO preservation of assets:** Courts have granted preliminary injunctions freezing RICO defendants' assets pending trial, particularly where dissipation risk is documented.

**Action item for counsel:** File lis pendens on all parcels identified in OWNERSHIP_PROVEN.md the same day the complaint is filed.
