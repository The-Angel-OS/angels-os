## Why This Letter Exists

CSC ServiceWorks is the third-party laundry vendor at both 2560 and 2566 Harn Blvd. Their app-only payment system locked Kenneth out of the dryer at the 2566 facility on the documented incident (clothes left soaking wet). The system runs on tenant-paid (rent-passed-through-master-meter) water. CSC collects revenue, pays the landlord a revenue share, pays zero for water — and locks tenants out when the app fails.

This letter serves three purposes:

1. **Preserve a documented complaint** with timestamps that survives any subsequent CSC log purge.
2. **Force CSC to disclose** the install dates, contract terms, and revenue-share arrangements with the Hilkert/Kistner LLCs (Luke Alexander LLC, H K Investment Co, Lady Lauren LLC, Lady Jane Investments LLC, Phirst LLC).
3. **Position CSC as a potential co-defendant** under joint-enterprise and unjust-enrichment theories, so their legal team prioritizes the response.

**Subject: Formal Notice of Service Failure, Water Cost-Shifting, and Demand for Cure — 2566 Harn Blvd Laundry Facility**

To CSC ServiceWorks Compliance / Customer Resolution:

I am filing a formal complaint and notice of intent regarding the laundry facility CSC ServiceWorks operates at **2566 Harn Blvd, Clearwater, FL 33764**. This letter is being preserved as evidence and will be incorporated into pending litigation against the property owner (Luke Alexander LLC / Douglas L. Hilkert, Manager).

**Incident:** On [DATE/TIME], I started a wash cycle at the on-site facility, paid through your app. When the cycle completed, the dryers were unreachable through the app. The machines did not appear on the network at all. My device — a Samsung S23 Ultra, Bluetooth enabled, fully provisioned, on a 5G connection — is not the failure point; multiple devices in the household have experienced identical failures with your system. My clothes were left soaking wet in a machine I had paid to use. I have screen recordings of the failure.

**Direct financial harm:**

1. **Wasted wash cycle** — paid for, unusable.
2. **Wet laundry hostage** — physical property locked in a machine I cannot operate.
3. **Forced relocation** — I had to transport wet laundry to an off-site laundromat to complete drying, incurring transportation cost, time loss, and equipment damage during the secondary trip.

**Water cost-shifting — the larger issue:**

The water supply at this facility is **shared and master-metered for the entire building at 2566 Harn Blvd**. Tenants pay for that water through rent. When your machines run, they consume water that all tenants in this building are collectively financially responsible for. CSC ServiceWorks pays nothing for that water. The property owner pockets a revenue share from CSC on every cycle. When your app locks me out of the machines I am subsidizing, **CSC ServiceWorks is collecting revenue from the in-building tenants who can connect, on water I am paying for, while denying me access.**

This is:

- A direct deceptive trade practice under **Florida Statute 501.201 et seq. (FDUTPA)**
- An unjust enrichment of CSC ServiceWorks and the property owner at this tenant's expense
- A material contributing factor in a habitability and retaliation claim against the property owner

**Demand:**

Within 14 days of this notice, CSC ServiceWorks shall:

1. **Refund all charges** to my account on file, with a full transaction history attached.
2. **Provide documentation** of the install date and contract terms for the machines at both 2560 Harn Blvd and 2566 Harn Blvd facilities, including any revenue-share or commission paid to Luke Alexander LLC, H K Investment Co, Lady Lauren LLC, Lady Jane Investments LLC, Phirst LLC, Douglas L. Hilkert, or Cynthia J. Kistner.
3. **Compensate** for the wasted cycle, the transportation cost of the relocated dry, and the documented time loss.
4. **Restore reliable coin-operated or universally-accessible service** to the 2566 Harn Blvd facility, or terminate the contract with this property owner.

If these items are not addressed within 14 days, this complaint will be filed with:

- The Florida Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division
- The Federal Trade Commission
- The Better Business Bureau (Tampa Bay region)
- The Clearwater Code Enforcement Division

I have preserved screen recordings, transaction history, and timestamped photos of the locked machines and the wet laundry.
