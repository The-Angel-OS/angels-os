## Why This Matters for the Case

### 1. Alter-Ego / Veil-Piercing Slam Dunk

The v2.3 brief alleged the LLC structure was a "mere instrumentality" of Doug Hilkert. **The Sunbiz records prove it:**

- The LLCs operate from Doug's law office (2557 Nursery Rd Suite A)
- Doug's law firm is the registered agent for both LUKE ALEXANDER LLC and HK INVESTMENT CO
- Doug is personally named as the Manager (Luke Alexander) and Director/President/Treasurer (HK Investment)
- His wife is the only other named officer
- There is no separate operational existence — these are pure paper entities

**Veil piercing is no longer a discovery question. It's a documented fact.**

### 2. Fraudulent Inducement / Failure to Disclose

A practicing real-estate attorney signing leases to disabled veterans through LLCs named after his children, while personally managing the LLCs from his law office, while concealing:
- The 1940s pre-code electrical infrastructure
- The pest ingress vector from decommissioned vents
- The master-meter water diversion scheme
- The HVAC degradation forcing 200-300% utility overcharges

...has committed **fraudulent inducement** under Florida common law (*Johnson v. Davis*, 480 So.2d 625). His attorney status creates a *heightened* disclosure duty.

### 3. Civil Conspiracy / Joint Enterprise (Doug + Cynthia)

Both siblings operate the same scheme across two adjoining buildings using the same playbook:
- Both use vanity-named LLCs (child names, family compound addresses)
- Both omit Hilkert from LLC names to evade an owner-name search
- Both share the parental 2160 Laurence Dr compound for registration purposes
- The Colliers listing markets all 20 family-controlled units as a single offering — proving coordinated business operation

**This is a textbook civil conspiracy claim.** Cynthia Kistner becomes a named co-defendant, not just a nominee. Her personal assets become exposed.

### 4. RICO / Pattern of Racketeering Activity

The 9-year, 20-unit, multi-LLC, dual-sibling operation with:
- Master-meter utility diversion (theft of services)
- App-only laundry conversion (current ongoing theft)
- Concealed structural defects causing personal injury (pest, electrical shock, child fall)
- Wire fraud (electronic rent collection from disabled veterans on Section 8)
- Retaliatory eviction practices when complaints surface

...meets the **predicate-act pattern requirement for Florida RICO (F.S. 895.03) and possibly federal RICO (18 USC § 1962).** Treble damages, attorney fees, and asset forfeiture available.

### 5. The Florida Bar Hammer Just Got Heavier

Doug isn't just an attorney who happens to own property. He's an attorney who **uses his law office** as the headquarters of the rental operation, **registers his shell LLCs to his firm's address**, and **uses his bar credentials** to obscure beneficial ownership through legally-structured but ethically fraudulent entities. Florida Bar Rule 4-8.4(c) (dishonesty, fraud, deceit, misrepresentation) applies directly to the use of his practice address as a shell-LLC headquarters.
