About this edition

The Holy Bible, offered as a canonical Work of The Angel OS in two public-domain translations: the modern **World English Bible (WEB)** and the classic **King James Version (KJV)**. Each translation is stored as its own edition file, so switching between them is the same simple gesture as switching languages — and future editions (other translations or languages) drop into the same slot.

This Work is the foundation of the daily reader, read-aloud, and the scripture reference spine that lets any Work in the Library cite a verse. The daily three-chapter reading feature was suggested by Ron — one of many gifts of the platform, freely given.
