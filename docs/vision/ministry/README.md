## What This Folder Is

The documents in this folder support the formation of a Florida nonprofit corporation that will pursue **religious, charitable, and educational** purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, growing organically out of the witnessing and community work that has been happening on the Clearwater Cruisin' YouTube channel and in the Pinellas County / Tampa Bay region since 2024.

The first concrete step is a conversation with **Father Pete at St. Alfred's Episcopal Church, Palm Harbor, FL** — a priest whose six-year quiet presence in the lead organizer's life (encountered at Enterprise Dog Park) has been a steady anchor of light. The ask is twofold:

1. **Spiritual discernment** — does this calling warrant becoming a formal ministry?
2. **Governance support** — would Father Pete (or a clergy delegate, or a Senior Warden) consider serving as one of the three required directors of the corporation?

The conversation is not transactional. It is the recognition of a relationship that has been forming for six years and a calling that has been forming for as many.

## The Mission, In Plain English

Clearwater Cruisin' Ministries exists to:

1. **Witness and serve** — bring attention, dignity, and material aid to people in Pinellas County and the wider Tampa Bay region who are too often overlooked: veterans, the elderly, the disabled, the housing-insecure, and those displaced by predatory landlords.

2. **Steward creation** — document and celebrate the marine wildlife, natural environments, and ordinary beauty of the Florida Gulf Coast, in the Episcopal tradition of *care for creation*. The dolphins, ospreys, manatees, and waters of this region are part of God's good creation and deserve attention, advocacy, and protection.

3. **Build housing trust capacity** — develop, in time, the institutional capability to acquire, rehabilitate, and steward residential property for the long-term benefit of veterans, low-income tenants, and others displaced by predatory housing practices. This work begins now, in pre-litigation form, with the Rainmaker case file documenting the pattern of harm in one Clearwater complex.

4. **Support veterans** — chaplaincy, advocacy, fellowship, and material assistance to veterans of all branches, with particular attention to those navigating disability ratings, housing instability, and reintegration.

5. **Foster community** — through media, public engagement, dog parks, the streets of Clearwater and Dunedin, the marinas, the sanctuaries, and the everyday encounters that make the Tampa Bay region a community worth witnessing.

## The Spiritual Frame — Julian of Norwich

"All shall be well, and all manner of things shall be well."

The 14th-century anchoress Julian of Norwich, whose work Father Pete preached on at the May 10, 2026 Mother's Day service at St. Alfred's, offers a theological grounding that maps with surprising precision to the work of this ministry:

- **Holy curiosity** — the discipline of seeking what God has placed in plain sight, rather than passing it by
- **Witnessing** — making oneself present to suffering and beauty alike, refusing to look away
- **The reorientation of attention** — training the inner gatekeeper (what neuroscientists call the Reticular Activating System and what Julian would have called *the cell of the soul*) to receive the world with reverence rather than fear
- **Confidence in ultimate restoration** — the conviction that despite the present "grit," the trajectory of God's creation is toward wholeness

The ministry is, in its essence, a practical expression of this Julian theology: walking the streets with a camera, sitting with veterans on the corner of Chestnut and Fort Harrison, watching ospreys at Dunedin Marina, documenting the conditions of one apartment building so that justice might eventually be done for its tenants — these are all forms of *bearing witness to the good*, in the face of conditions that invite despair.

## Working Name Options

The legal name should be chosen before filing. Current options under consideration:

1. **Clearwater Cruisin' Ministries, Inc.** — natural continuation of existing brand identity; immediately recognizable to current channel audience
2. **All Shall Be Well Ministries, Inc.** — Julian-inspired; theologically grounded; less tied to a single media channel
3. **Sky Cousins Ministry, Inc.** — emphasizes care-for-creation dimension; uses the natural-mystical framing from the wildlife-documentation work
4. **Pinellas Ministries of Witness, Inc.** — more traditional Episcopal naming convention; emphasizes regional rootedness
5. **The Fifth Element Ministry, Inc.** — personal/relational naming; emphasizes partnership

A combined approach is also possible — e.g., filing as `All Shall Be Well Ministries, Inc.` and operating publicly as `Clearwater Cruisin' Ministries` under a d/b/a registration.

Final selection to be made after the Father Pete conversation and ideally with his input.
