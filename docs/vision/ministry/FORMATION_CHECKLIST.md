## Phase 5 — IRS Form 1023 or 1023-EZ (Week 2-3 for prep, then 2-4 weeks IRS turnaround for EZ; 3-6+ months for full)

### Decision: Which Form?

**Form 1023-EZ — RECOMMENDED for initial filing**
- **Cost:** $275
- **Eligibility:** Annual gross receipts projected under $50,000 for first 3 years; total assets under $250,000
- **Time to prepare:** 2-4 hours
- **IRS turnaround:** Typically 2-4 weeks
- **Best for:** Initial formation; the ministry can later upgrade if it grows beyond these thresholds

**Form 1023 (Long Form)**
- **Cost:** $600
- **Eligibility:** All other organizations
- **Time to prepare:** 20-40 hours (or $1,500-$5,000 to outsource to a nonprofit attorney)
- **IRS turnaround:** 3-6+ months
- **Best for:** Organizations expecting larger initial operations or holding significant real property

**Recommendation:** File 1023-EZ initially. Upgrade later if needed.

### Form 1023-EZ Eligibility Worksheet

Complete this checklist before filing:

- [ ] Annual gross receipts projected under $50,000 for first 3 years? **YES required**
- [ ] Total assets under $250,000? **YES required**
- [ ] Not a church, hospital, school, or supporting organization? **NOT-CHURCH required for EZ** — *note: this is a tricky one. Ministries can file EZ if they DON'T self-identify as a "church" in the IRS technical sense (i.e., a worshipping congregation with regular services). The "ministry" framing is fine; the "church" framing requires the long form. Consult counsel.*
- [ ] Not a private operating foundation? **Confirmed NO**
- [ ] Not formed under the laws of a foreign country? **Confirmed NO (Florida)**

If all YES → 1023-EZ. If any NO → consult counsel.

### Filing 1023-EZ

- [ ] Create account at https://www.pay.gov/public/form/start/62759871
- [ ] Complete online form (most fields prepopulate from your application data)
- [ ] Pay $275 filing fee
- [ ] Submit
- [ ] Receive Determination Letter typically within 2-4 weeks

**Phase 5 gate:** IRS Determination Letter received (this is the official 501(c)(3) document)

## Key Risks To Manage

1. **Address selection** — Do NOT use 2566 Harn Blvd Unit 13 as principal office address. The pending Rainmaker matter makes the property address inappropriate as the ministry's headquarters. Use a parishioner address, the Curlew Road parish address (with permission), or a Florida registered-agent service address.

2. **"Church" vs. "Ministry" framing** — If the IRS classifies this as a "church" rather than a "ministry," different rules apply (automatic exemption but limitations on Form 1023-EZ). The Episcopal context makes this a real question. Counsel review is recommended.

3. **Personal-asset commingling** — Once the bank account is open, all ministry-related expenses must go through the ministry account, and no personal expenses should hit the ministry card. Clean separation from day one is non-negotiable for 501(c)(3) compliance.

4. **Lobbying restriction** — The Corporation cannot endorse candidates or do substantial political lobbying. The Rainmaker case is allowed (litigation in furtherance of the housing-justice purpose is permitted), but Corporation should NOT endorse, e.g., specific Clearwater City Council candidates.

5. **Founder-as-only-director** — Florida requires 3 directors, but the IRS also looks for **independent** governance. Having Tyler + a third party who is NOT a household member is best practice. Father Pete or a Senior Warden ideally; failing that, a non-related parishioner or a community leader.
