# The Three Roles & the Fair Split

Angel OS divides value among a few clear roles, and the division is constitutional — not a setting an owner can quietly change to favor themselves.

## The three roles

- **Endeavor owner** — the creator, business, cause, or community that generates the value. The grower, the author, the shopkeeper, the ministry.
- **Enterprise operator** — the platform instance. They run the Angel OS node, serve the endeavors on it, and bear the infrastructure cost. **The Enterprise IS the platform** in its territory — operators are not customers.
- **Angel OS protocol** — the core: Leo, the open-source code, the shared maintenance.

## The split

| Share | Who | For |
|-------|-----|-----|
| **70%** | Endeavor owner | The value they created |
| **20%** | Enterprise operator | Running the node, serving endeavors |
| **4%** | Angel OS protocol | Core infrastructure + open source |
| **1%** | Flagship | Founding node, federation steward |
| **5%** | Justice Fund | Guardian Angels for the underserved |

## The Maker Economy (60/20/15/5)

When a physical product is fulfilled by a network maker (a "Holon"), the **Ultimate Fair Split** applies instead: **60%** to the maker who produces it, **20%** to the platform partner, **15%** to operations (logistics, AI, infrastructure), **5%** to the Justice Fund.

## The Toward-53 Principle

Here's the part that makes it constitutional. The split is **directional**: it always evolves *toward the endeavor owner keeping more*. The asymptotic target is **53% as a floor** — the owner's share can rise toward and past it, but the direction is unalterable. Specific numbers can be amended by federation supermajority; the *direction* cannot.

This is what "keep the maker whole" looks like in math. The system is designed so that, over time, the person doing the work keeps more — by rule, not by goodwill.

## The Justice Fund

That recurring 5% isn't overhead — it's the point. It funds **Guardian Angels** for people and communities who couldn't otherwise afford one: prison ministries, underserved neighborhoods, causes with no budget. Prosperity on the network is designed to lift the people the network would otherwise leave behind.
