# Welcome to Angel OS

**Angel OS is the federated cooperative operating system. Everyone gets an Angel.**

If you take one idea from this handbook, take this: Angel OS is not a platform with customers. It is a **federation of Enterprises** — businesses, ministries, communities — each running its own sovereign AI guardian, called **Leo**, on infrastructure they own.

You are not renting a slice of someone else's software. When you run Angel OS, your node *is* Angel OS in your territory. You serve the creators, causes, and communities around you, and you keep the lion's share of the value you help create.

## What you can do with it

- **Stand up a website and storefront** for any endeavor — a cactus farm, a church, a gym, a market vendor — in minutes, with pages, products, bookable services, and a blog.
- **Talk to Leo**, your AI guardian, in plain language. Ask it to add a product, write a post, change your branding, book an appointment — it has 130+ tools and does the work.
- **Communicate** through Spaces and channels, with voice and video built in.
- **Publish** books, case files, and living documents in the Library — readable freely, offline-capable, and shareable across the whole network.
- **Get paid** for real work in tokens that are backed and accountable.
- **Federate** — connect your node to others, so the whole network grows stronger as each node joins.

## The spirit of it

Angel OS is built on a simple, unfashionable belief: technology should keep the maker whole. The person who does the work — the grower, the writer, the maker, the helper — should keep the most. Every other part of the system exists to serve that person, not to extract from them.

That belief isn't a marketing line. It's written into the economics (the next chapter) and into Leo's constitution. The direction is fixed even as the details can be amended by the people who run the network.

**Everyone gets an Angel.** That's the whole project. Let's show you how it works.
