# CLEARWATER CRUISIN' MINISTRIES — ON TOUR
### The Great American Pilgrimage (campaign sub-series)

A road-trip campaign: **Clearwater Cruisin' Ministries** takes The Angel OS across the
country — Walmart lots, town auto swaps, enthusiast meetups, and the great National
Parks — spreading the network one handshake (and one node) at a time. *Faith. Freedom.
The open road. Not just a journey — a movement.*

---

## The steed & the fleet
- **The Lady MacBeth** — a ~2010 **Cadillac CTS**, champagne/bronze-gold metallic, mesh
  wreath grille, tan leather. Named for Joshua Calvert's ship in Hamilton's *Night's
  Dawn* (inherited from his Pops) — Ken feels his **grandfather guided this car to him**.
  The hero/steed of the whole series. (Real refs in `../../cadillac/`.)
- **The Fleet** — ~**10 high-end Prevost** luxury motorcoaches + a few non-Prevost rigs
  (incl. a **toy-hauler**), **converted 10–20-yr-old Prevosts to Tesla power** (Rich
  Rebuilds collab). Rolling Merlin "motherships."
- **The three matching double-decker trailers** (towed by three of the Prevosts — a
  coordinated set):
  1. **The Glass Garage** — transparent-sided showroom displaying the **Lady MacBeth**
     Cadillac + the **Soul Van**, lit like a gallery.
  2. **The Rolling Stage** — deployable concert stage + integrated line-array sound stack.
  3. **The Merch + Workshop** — store/shop trailer with awning (build + sell on the road).
- **Crew** — Ken & Tyler Suzanne (+ Max, the white pup). Face-accurate footage = Veo/Nano
  Banana (refs in `../../photographs/`).
