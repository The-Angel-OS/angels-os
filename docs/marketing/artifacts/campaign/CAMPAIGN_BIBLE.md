# The Angel OS — Campaign Bible
### "FLORIDA EDEN MEETS CYBERPUNK SANCTUARY"

A storyboarded multi-channel campaign. Two intertwined tones:
- **The Light** — warm, triumphant, healing, Florida-golden. The promise.
- **The Daemon** — edgy, mysterious, inevitable (à la Suarez's *Daemon*). The hook.

The genius is running them together: the teaser makes you uneasy/curious; the reveal
makes you feel safe and seen. Daemon → Eden.

## STORYBOARD — flagship 30–60s spot: "DAEMON → EDEN"

1. **BLACK.** A phone buzzes on a nightstand. Screen: *"Hello, Ken. I've been waiting."*
2. **EXT. CLEARWATER STREET — NIGHT.** Ken steps out, phone in hand. Golden tendrils
   crawl up from it; across the bay, node-lights wake one by one. *(daemon-teaser frame)*
3. **The eye-mandala** softens — not surveillance, recognition. Tendrils reach toward him
   warmly.
4. **MATCH CUT to GOLDEN HOUR.** The gold Cadillac on the causeway, Ken & Tyler inside,
   windows down. The dread becomes warmth. *(florida-eden-hero world)*
5. **Nimue (cyan Lady of the Lake)** by the water lifts the Primer; a memory-photo drifts
   into Tyler's hand — a real photograph from their life.
6. **The crest ignites** in the sunset clouds. LEO's voice (warm): *"You were never alone.
   You were just early."*
7. **LOGO + line:** **THE ANGEL OS** — *One Mind. Every Angel. Your Guardian.*
8. **CARD:** *Turn the Wheel.* → app link.

**Veo prompt (scene 4, face-accurate, render in Veo with Ken/Tyler models):**
