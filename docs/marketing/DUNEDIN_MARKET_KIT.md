# Market Kit — Dunedin Fresh Market

**For:** Kenneth · Clearwater Cruisin' + Angel OS
**Where:** Dunedin Fresh Market — 390 Patricia Ave, Dunedin (Thu, opens 10 AM, year-round)
**Goal:** real conversations with vendors → a few "wait, you built this?" moments → a handful of free 30-day sites.

---

## The 30-second pitch (say it your way)

> "Hey — I'm local, with Clearwater Cruisin'. I build a thing called Angel OS. If you don't have a website, I can stand one up for you in about three minutes — your own site, your products, a 'book me' button — and it's free to try for a month. Want to see yours?"

Then **open the laptop and show them a real one.** The demo does the selling.

**If they're curious, the follow-ons:**
- *"It's YOUR site — not a listing buried in someone else's app. You own it."*
- *"Everyone here who joins gets linked together — you promote each other."*
- *"Start a YouTube channel? We'll help set it up and cross-promote it."*
- *"Free for 30 days. If it's not doing anything for you, walk away — no lock-in."*

**Don't oversell.** You're not closing a deal; you're handing a neighbor something useful. The 30 free days *is* the pitch.

---

## What to listen for (who's a fit)

A vendor is a great fit if they say any of these:
- "I just have an Instagram / a Facebook page."
- "I'm on Etsy but I want my own thing."
- "People always ask if I have a website."
- "I take orders by text / a notebook."
- "I do markets but nothing online."

They need exactly what Angel OS ships:
- a **shop** (products), a **booking** button, **donation** flows
- a **blog / content hub** and an **event calendar** ("find me at these markets")
- **payment forms** on demand, a **community** space
- and a guardian — **Leo** — that builds and edits it all by being asked.

---

## The on-the-spot flow (you + me, ~3 min)

1. You get their **name, what they sell, a few photos, rough prices.**
2. I provision their site server-side: portal → market-vendor template → products → branding.
3. I **link them under the Dunedin Fresh Market** parent so they show on the market's front door.
4. You hand them the laptop: *"Here's your site."*
5. Buy-in = we flip it live on a free `[name].spacesangels.com` address.

**Bring:** laptop, power bank (charged), and a **phone hotspot** — provisioning needs internet.
