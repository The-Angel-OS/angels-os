## Do NOT claim on this call

The fastest way to fail a test is one oversold line.

- ❌ Any FDA clearance or approval language. Registered only.
- ❌ That SEO rankings transfer on a DNS flip. They mostly do with the paths
  preserved, but it is not a guarantee and it is not instant.
- ❌ Fulfillment, shipping labels, or inventory sync with whoever ships his
  belts — not built, not investigated.
- ❌ That email marketing is running for him. The drip engine exists on the
  platform; nothing is configured for Kessela.
- ❌ Anything about ad performance. We have not run a dollar for him.
- ❌ "It's finished." It sells and it's his brand. Copy is still his verbatim
  wording, and he hasn't approved a word of it.
