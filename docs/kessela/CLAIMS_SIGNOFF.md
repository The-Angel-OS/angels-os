## The one fact everything hangs on

**Kessela is a REGISTERED Class II medical device.** Registration is a listing
with the FDA. It is **not** clearance (510(k)) and **not** approval. A cleared
device may state what it treats. A registered one may not.

That single distinction decides every line below. It is not a technicality
someone can decide to ignore — it is the difference between marketing and a
regulatory problem, and the exposure sits on David's company, not on ours.

## §C — NOT proposed, and why

Deliberately absent. Not oversight.

| Not saying | Why |
|---|---|
| Treats, cures, corrects or resolves incontinence | Requires clearance. Registration is not clearance. |
| "Clinically proven to…" any outcome | Currently on kessela.com. Needs a specific study for a specific claim, and we do not have the papers yet. |
| Reduces fat · shrinks fat cells · "equal to 300 sit-ups" | On the live site today. Body-composition claims are the most litigated in this category. |
| Before-and-after photographs | **Meta bans them outright.** Not a compliance preference — creative built on them cannot run as paid. |
| Replaces physiotherapy or surgery | A substitution claim, and a medical one. |
| Any specific timeframe for results | Implies an outcome. |

**Worth David hearing plainly:** three of these are on his site right now. We are
not adding them to the new page, and at some point the existing pages should be
brought into line — that is a separate decision and his to make.
