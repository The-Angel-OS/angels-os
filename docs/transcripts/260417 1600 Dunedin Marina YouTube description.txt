DESCRIPTION

🌅🐬 Afternoon at Dunedin Marina—explorations & observations, April 17 2026. We're parked, walking the waterfront, and vibing with Zac Brown Band (Sweet Annie) on the track. Baby birds in a nest overhead, dogs Daisy, Mika & Max in tow, and the usual clear intention: love the water, the people, and maybe catch dolphins—they often come in close right here (including a three-minute show around a tour boat a day or two before this run).

Conversations: visitors from Chicago; a real welcome to Flow Ride; Clearwater Cruisin' as the channel; dolphins, architecture, and church-safe vs clearly-marked content. Longer talk with a gentleman who worked in schools and on the Genesis student information system (cargo-cult architecture, adapting a big project with a buddy). Tips for e-bike riders: consider a 13T→11T rear cassette for easier high-speed pedaling. Chat with a marina staffer: City of Dunedin Marina, Dunedin Sailing Club, buildings from the late 1920s / dedicated 1931, hurricane history, and the story of the fill and brickwork along the water—old-looking but enduring.

🔔 If you used to service fire extinguishers at this marina and the big hotel in the background—this one's for you. The marina holds a lot of history; every walk adds another page.
