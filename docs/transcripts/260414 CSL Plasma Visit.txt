A real, unfiltered visit to **CSL Plasma in Largo** (East Bay Drive location) with my roommate Jason.

This video shows the actual process of donating plasma, the friendly and professional staff, and casual conversation about how the donation works, how much you can earn, and why it's a meaningful way to help save lives while making some extra money.

We talk about using referral codes, the experience of being a regular donor (I've now donated 34+ times), and the positive atmosphere at this particular center. The staff here — including Rachel, Mindy, Onja, Ramon, Grace, and many others — consistently make the process smooth, respectful, and even enjoyable.

This is what everyday service and community look like in Clearwater / Pinellas County — real people helping real people.

No script. No edits. Just real life, real donation, and real gratitude.
