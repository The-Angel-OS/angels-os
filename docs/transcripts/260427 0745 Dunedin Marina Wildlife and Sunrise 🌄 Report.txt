Our office this morning: Dunedin Marina Inlet, 7:45am, 8K motion-stabilized, three dogs, at least two manatees.

That's the Clearwater Cruisin' commute.

We talk a lot about building things — Angel OS, the Neimu app, a photo inventory module that lets you photograph your stock and let AI do the cataloguing. But the honest truth is, all of that gets built from the same place we do everything else: outside, at the water, with the dogs, cameras rolling.

This morning someone at the boat ramp said "what an idyllic lifestyle." And honestly — yeah. It cost $150 in gas to get here and we're running on food pantry supplies, but the manatee showed up anyway and the sun came up over the marina and the Ospreys are out there feeding their fledglings and the Great Horned Owls are watching from the tree line.

You build from where you are. We're building from here.

A few things worth noting from this morning's session:

→ Neimu (the Angel OS client app) is in active development. Photo inventory is the first killer feature — photograph your shelf, get a structured inventory update. Designed for small business owners, collectors, anyone managing physical assets.

→ The Telepathy Tapes podcast is genuinely worth your time. NPR-quality. Mind-expanding. Go listen.

→ The Dunedin Marina is on a ley line. Multiple, actually. Not a metaphor.
