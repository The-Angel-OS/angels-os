Assignment Overview
Make an appointment for a drug screening at an assigned location to evaluate the drug screen collection process and the employee’s adherence to the strict procedure for collecting a urine sample for drug screening. You will be assigned 1 of 2 scenarios (full sample or shy bladder), which dictates the amount of urine to provide during the drug screening. This document provides detailed instructions and a questionnaire for a mystery shopping assignment evaluating the DOT pre-employment drug screening process at Quest Diagnostics.

### **Core Assignment Details**

  * **Objective**: Evaluate the drug screen collection process and employee adherence to strict urine sample collection procedures.
  * **Scenarios**: Shoppers are assigned either a "Full Sample" scenario (providing sufficient urine) or a "Shy Bladder" scenario (providing little to no urine) to test the collector's response.
  * **Identity**: Shoppers act as drivers for the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA).

### **General Requirements**

  * **Eligibility**: Shoppers or their immediate family must never have worked for Quest Diagnostics. Participants must not test positive for illegal or controlled substances.
  * **Required Items**: A printed test requisition form and a valid photo ID or driver's license are mandatory.
  * **Restrictions**: Photos inside the location are strictly prohibited.
  * **Reporting**: Reports must be submitted online within 12 hours of completing the assignment.

### **Key Collection Procedures to Observe**

  * **Restroom Security**: Shoppers must confirm the water is turned off, the toilet water is colored (blue), and no adulterants (like soap) are accessible.
  * **Sample Handling**: The collector must maintain a line of sight with the sample until it is sealed. The collector should check the sample temperature within four minutes.
  * **Sealing and Signing**: The collector must pour the sample into two bottles and seal them in front of the shopper. Shoppers must initial the seals only after they are on the bottles and sign the Donor Certification statement last.
  * **Employee Interaction**: Shoppers must record the names of employees at registration and the collector, noting their courtesy, greeting, and professionalism.

### **Questionnaire Components**

The evaluation covers several categories:

  * **Arrival Experience**: Wait time, number of people in the waiting area, and the clarity of sign-in procedures.
  * **Collection Process**: Verification of ID, pocket checks, hand washing, and explanation of the process.
  * **Interaction and Observations**: Cleanliness of the bathroom, staff behaviors (positive or negative), and whether any patient health information was overheard.
